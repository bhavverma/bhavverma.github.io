## About

I am a **PhD student at the University of Toronto** working at the intersection of machine learning, bioinformatics, and digital health. My work focuses on developing robust computational methods that translate complex biological and clinical data into practical tools for healthcare research.

Before starting my PhD, I completed my **MSc at the University of Toronto**, where I developed [HiTaxon](https://github.com/ParkinsonLab/HiTaxon), and my **BSc at York University**, where I contributed to [TetraMANIA](https://tetramania.bradley.edu/).

## Research Interests

- Machine learning for biomedical and clinical applications  
- Metagenomics and microbial community analysis  
- Scalable taxonomic classification and reference database design  
- Digital health and remote patient monitoring  
- Reliable, interpretable models for real-world clinical decision support
